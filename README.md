## 🛠️ General Installation & Requirements

1. **Keymaster & Licensing**: All paid resources are granted automatically to your Cfx.re / Keymaster account upon purchase via Tebex.
2. **Dependencies**: All D3XL resources are **100% standalone** and optimized (0.00ms idle CPU usage).
3. **Installation Procedure**:
   - Download the resource folder from Keymaster or GitHub.
   - Place inside your server's `resources/` folder.
   - Add `ensure resource_name` to your `server.cfg`.
